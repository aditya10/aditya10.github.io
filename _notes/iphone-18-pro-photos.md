Here are some of my favourite photographs captured so far on the iPhone 18 Pro.

I find the photographic styles paired with the texture control to be really capable at making iPhone photos look unique and have richer, more contrasty colours. I find the grain to be too heavy-handed, and I'd like to see an update where we can control the amount of grain we want to add to the images.

The aperture control on iPhone 18 Pro is not useful in all situations, but I find myself reaching for it a lot when I am shooting pictures with a purpose... the f/4 gives you the sun stars, and the f/1.48 provides really nice background separation, especially when shooting at 2x zoom.
