Injecting small VLMs with strategies for tool-use on videos

Small VLMs are quite capable at tool use. I am working on a problem where we want to use tool-use agentic VLMs to study videos generated by T2V models, and identify errors in them (side note: we worked on Spotlight, a benchmark for local video generation error detection). I provide the model with tools for viewing frames from videos at higher fps, showing frames side-by-side for comparisons, zooming into different frame regions, and even optical flow based frame motion comparison. 

Here’s something interesting I found: models like Qwen 3.5 VL 9B are absolutely capable of tool-use, but they are hampered by their inability to reason about the problem well (side note: see this [embedding code provided below] Reddit post about how bad Qwen’s thinking is), come up with hypotheses to test, at strategically use tools. 

**Research Question**

If a bigger model, say Opus 5.5 or GPT-6 Sol, could provide the Qwen model with a strategy, can this strategy be injected into Qwen’s multi-turn conversation to improve tool use?

The short answer is **YES**! In fact, it produced some rather interesting outcomes… not only did Qwen become more accurate at tool-use, but its reasoning traces on the video were shorter, more succinct and more confident. This side effect is interesting: by modifying the expectation of how the Qwen model should think about the video, it started adapting that thinking setting to all future turns.
